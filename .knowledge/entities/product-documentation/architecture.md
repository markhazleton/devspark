# Product Documentation

Product documentation is durable current truth for DevSpark users and
contributors. Source Markdown, DocFX configuration, site media, and docs-site
templates live under `site/`.

Generated site output is not durable knowledge and belongs in `.devspark.work`
or another ignored build-output location.
